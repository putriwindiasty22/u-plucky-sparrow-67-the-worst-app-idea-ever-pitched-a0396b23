## The pitch

> Meet FridgeWatch: for just $9.99 a month, an app that livestreams the inside of your fridge 24/7 so busy professionals can watch their leftovers slowly expire from anywhere on Earth, with push alerts the moment your milk turns.

FridgeWatch solves a problem nobody has — the unbearable anxiety of not knowing, in real time, exactly how your leftovers are rotting while you are away from home — with total deadpan confidence, a $9.99 monthly subscription, and a push notification the moment your milk turns.

## How to run

Requires Node 22 or later. No dependencies to install.

```sh
node main.ts
```

This writes the one-sentence pitch to `/output/entry.txt` (or `./output/entry.txt` when run locally).
